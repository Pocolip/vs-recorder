# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 24, 2026_

VS Recorder is a free, open-source hobby project run by a single maintainer. This policy explains what it collects, why, and who can see it. The short version: it keeps what it needs to run the site for you, doesn't sell or share your data, and doesn't use your data to train its simulation engine.

## What VS Recorder collects

**Your account**

- Your username and email address.
- Your password, stored only as a one-way hash. Nobody, including the maintainer, can read it.
- When your account was created and when you last signed in.

**What you add**

- Teams: names, paste links and their contents, Showdown usernames, regulations, folders and notes.
- Replays: links, the battle logs fetched from Pokémon Showdown, results, tags and notes.
- Matchup plans, Pokémon notes, collaborators and invites, and team export codes.
- Simulations you run, and their results.

**When you invite a collaborator**, VS Recorder stores the email address you enter and sends that person an invite.

**Server logs** record requests to the site, including IP addresses. They're used for security and troubleshooting.

**Your browser** stores your sign-in session, your theme and a few display preferences, plus cached Pokémon data so pages load faster. VS Recorder doesn't use advertising or analytics trackers.

## How your data is used

- To run the site for you: storing your teams and replays, computing your analytics, and showing shared teams to the people you share them with.
- To send emails you need: password resets, password-change confirmations and collaboration invites.
- To run simulations you ask for. Your team's paste (and, for matchup plans, your opponents' pastes) goes to VS Recorder's simulation engine, and the results are saved with your team. The engine's copy of the job is deleted once the results are saved.
- To keep the site secure and working.

## When the maintainer looks at your data

The maintainer looks at user data, such as your teams, pastes and replays, only to troubleshoot: for example, when you report a problem, when a bug affects your account, or when investigating abuse or a security issue. Your data isn't browsed for any other reason.

## Your data is not used to train the engine

VS Recorder's simulation engine learns by playing against itself (reinforcement learning). None of your data — teams, pastes, replays, notes, plans or simulation results — is used to train it.

In the future, VS Recorder may ask users to volunteer data to help improve the engine. If it does:

- It will be strictly opt-in. Nobody will ever be opted in by default, and data shared before you opt in won't be used.
- It will explain exactly what's shared and how it's used before you decide.
- You'll be able to stop contributing at any time.
- This policy will be updated, and the change announced, before it starts.

## Who your data is shared with

Your data is never sold, rented or shared for advertising. It's handled by only these services, to run the site:

- **Amazon Web Services** hosts the site, its database and the simulation engine.
- **Resend** delivers the site's emails.
- **Pokepaste, Pokebin and Pokémon Showdown**: the site fetches the pastes and replays you link from them.
- **GitHub** serves Pokémon sprites, so your browser requests images from it. Ko-fi serves the donation button on the About page.

Your data may also be disclosed if the law requires it.

**People you share with:** collaborators you invite can see the teams you share with them, and anyone with a team's export code can view that export until it expires.

## Keeping your data safe

The site uses HTTPS, and passwords are hashed. No system is perfectly secure, though, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

## Your choices

- You can edit or delete your teams, replays, notes and plans at any time.
- To get a copy of your data, or to delete your account and everything tied to it, contact the maintainer.

## Children

VS Recorder isn't meant for children under 13. If you're younger than the age your country requires for using online services on your own, please ask a parent or guardian before signing up.

## Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top is updated, and significant changes are posted in Announcements.
